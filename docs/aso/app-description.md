## Full Description (2,992 chars — comfortably under the 4,000 limit)

```
Meet Ripple.

She's a water otter who floats peacefully when you're calm — and tucks into her shell when the day gets heavy. Ripple doesn't show you a stressful number. She shows you how you feel.

StressMonitor turns your body's signals into a companion you can actually understand. No clinical scores. No anxiety-inducing dashboards. Just a friend who reflects your stress back to you, gently, in real time.

THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE CALM
Ripple's mood isn't a guess. It's driven by a genuine 5-factor stress algorithm that reads real data from Apple Health:
• Heart Rate Variability (HRV) — how resilient your body is right now
• Resting Heart Rate — compared to your personal baseline
• Sleep Quality — because rest is everything
• Physical Activity — separating good energy from stress
• Recovery — how well you bounce back

The more you use StressMonitor, the better it knows you. Your personal baseline adapts over 30 days, so the readings get smarter — not more stressful.

5 COMPANIONS, NOT 5 DASHBOARDS
Collect a family of elemental creatures who each reflect a part of your wellbeing:
• Ripple — the Water Otter (relaxation & calm)
• Blossom — the Forest Fox (growth & resilience)
• Ember — the Fire Phoenix (energy & motivation)
• Zephyr — the Cloud Rabbit (breathing & mindfulness)
• Lumi — the Moon Owl (sleep & wisdom)

Each character evolves as you build healthy habits. Grow them from their first form all the way to their majestic final stage. They change with your stress, they grow with your consistency — and they make checking in feel like coming home.

BREATHE IT OUT
Feeling tense? Tap into guided box breathing (4-4-4-4) and watch Ripple settle back to calm as you slow your rhythm. Three-minute sessions, real feedback, real relief.

BUILT FOR APPLE WATCH
Your companion lives on your wrist. The Apple Watch app works entirely on its own — measure your stress, check your character, and breathe, all without your phone. Home-screen widgets and complications keep Ripple a glance away.

YOUR PRIVACY IS THE POINT
StressMonitor is privacy-first, and we mean it:
• Everything runs on your device. Your health data never leaves it.
• Optional iCloud sync is end-to-end encrypted — even Apple can't read it.
• No third-party analytics. No advertising. No tracking. No accounts.
• Export or erase your data anytime. It's yours.

The only thing that ever leaves your device is fully anonymized chat context for the optional AI assistant — never your health readings.

DESIGNED FOR EVERYONE
StressMonitor supports VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, and full accessibility. Ripple's state is announced, not just shown. Stress-aware, color-blind friendly, and calm by design.

StressMonitor is a wellness and mindfulness tool. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose any condition, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional for medical concerns.

No scores. No noise. Just calm.
Download StressMonitor and meet your companion today.
```
