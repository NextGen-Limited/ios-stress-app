## The universal elevator pitch

> StressMonitor reads five physiological signals — HRV, heart rate, sleep, activity, and recovery — learns your personal baseline over 30 days, and shows you the result as a **character whose face changes with your stress level**. No stress scores, no number that judges you. Just a companion that reflects how you're doing, with breathing exercises and a mini-walk built in for when you need to act on it.

---

## Niche elevator pitches

### ME/CFS

> If your energy crashes and recovery are the center of your day, an HRV-only monitor misses the story. StressMonitor treats **sleep and recovery as first-class signals** alongside HRV and heart rate, so the picture reflects the energy/stress balance you actually live with. It shows you a face, not a score — because for energy-limited conditions, the last thing you need is a number telling you you're failing.

**Lead with:** recovery + sleep as first-class factors · no scores · privacy.

### ADHD

> Stress with ADHD isn't about one bad reading — it's the spikes from overwhelm, RSD, and task-switching, and the challenge is *noticing the pattern* without spiraling into shame about it. StressMonitor shows your state as a **character expression, not a score**, so awareness feels like companionship, not a leaderboard. Built-in breathing exercises and a mini-walk give you something to *do* in the moment the face looks tense.

**Lead with:** no-scores/faces · awareness without judgment · in-the-moment breathing.

### Burnout

> Burnout is slow, systemic stress — the kind that shows up in wrecked sleep, missing recovery, and a creeping baseline you stop noticing. StressMonitor's 5-factor model (HRV + heart rate + **sleep + activity + recovery**) maps the slow burn over weeks, not just the daily spike. The character evolves as you build healthy streaks — recovery is rewarded, never punished.

**Lead with:** whole-physiology picture over weeks · recovery rewarded, not punished · breathing + walk.

## Comparison: StressMonitor vs StressWatch

The honest version. StressWatch is a proven, 5M-user product. We don't out-polish them — we out-niche them on algorithm breadth and philosophy.

### Head-to-head

| | **StressMonitor** | **StressWatch** |
|---|---|---|
| **Stress factors** | **5** — HRV + Heart Rate + Sleep + Activity + Recovery | 2 — HRV + Resting Heart Rate |
| **Score display** | **No scores — faces only** (core principle) | Numeric stress score |
| **Characters** | **5** with 3-stage evolution (Ripple, Blossom, Ember, Zephyr, Lumi) | 1 mascot (Mr.Fizz) |
| **Breathing exercises** | **Yes** — box breathing (4-4-4-4) + biofeedback | No |
| **Mini Walk exercise** | **Yes** | No |
| **Morning Readiness** | **Yes** — HRV trend analysis | No |
| **Cloud sync** | **Yes** — E2E-encrypted CloudKit | No (on-device only) |
| **Home Screen widgets** | **Yes** — Small/Medium/Large + Lock Screen | No |
| **Data export** | **Yes** — CSV/JSON | No |
| **Privacy / analytics** | **Zero** third-party analytics; HealthKit read-only | Firebase analytics |
| **AI coach** | On-device Apple Intelligence + cloud (privacy-routed) | Cloud "AI Health Coach" |
| **Users** | Pre-launch | 5M+ |
| **App Store rating** | — | 4.7★ (20K reviews) |
| **Bio Age** | No | **Yes** (gamification hook) |
| **Custom watch faces** | WidgetKit complications | **Yes** (photos/GIFs) |

### Where StressMonitor wins (lead with these)

1. **5 factors vs 2.** For CFS/burnout, recovery and sleep aren't extras — they're the story. StressWatch can't see them.
2. **No scores — faces only.** The single biggest emotional differentiator. StressWatch hands you a number; we hand you a face.
3. **5 characters with evolution.** Recovery is rewarded, never punished — a companionship loop, not a gamification treadmill.
4. **Breathing + Mini Walk live next to the reading.** Most apps stop at "you're stressed." We give you something to do about it.
5. **E2E-encrypted cloud sync + zero analytics.** For communities who are private about health data, "nothing leaves your devices" is a headline, not a footnote.

### Where StressWatch still wins (be honest)

1. **5M users, 4.7★, Apple-featured in 171 countries.** Social proof we can't touch yet.
2. **Bio Age** — a strong gamification hook we don't have.
3. **Custom watch faces** (photos/GIFs behind the mascot) — a delightful feature we lack.
4. **Regional pricing & lifetime deals** — mature monetization we haven't built.

> **How to use this honestly:** Never say "better than StressWatch." Say "different on purpose — 5 factors and no scores, built for people who want the whole picture without the number." Let the reader conclude the rest.

## The "no scores" framing (use everywhere)

This is our signature. When asked "how stressed am I?", most apps answer with a number. We answer with a face. The five tiers:

| Tier | Face | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Resting | 😴 | Recovered, low physiological load |
| Calm | 😊 | Settled, regulated |
| Balanced | 😐 | Normal day-to-day |
| Tense | 😰 | Elevated stress — time to notice |
| Overwhelmed | 🐚 | High load — consider an intervention |

> "The character **is** the stress indicator. There's no score hidden behind it. That's the whole point."

## Privacy statement (boilerplate, for any data question)

> StressMonitor stores everything locally in on-device encrypted storage and syncs via end-to-end-encrypted iCloud (CloudKit) — we can't read your health data, and neither can anyone else. We read HealthKit (HRV + heart rate + sleep + activity) but never write to it. There are no third-party analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking. The AI coach routes only anonymized chat context; your health data stays on-device.
