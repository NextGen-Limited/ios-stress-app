<objective>
Close verification gap 2 (CR-01): separate purchased credits from the free allotment so the monthly reset cannot destroy paid value. Today `resetMonthlyCredits` runs `set total_credits = 50, used_credits = 0 where plan_type = 'free'` (src/lib/cron.ts:4-11) while `redeemCredits` adds purchased credits to that SAME `total_credits` column (src/lib/credits.ts:95-99) — a free-tier user's remaining purchased credits are hard-reset to 50 on the 1st of every month. Direct paid-value data loss on the phase's headline feature, contradicting user-confirmed DEC-2 ("one-time top-ups").

Purpose: Without this, every pack purchase by a free-tier user is partially confiscated at each month boundary — an unacceptable money-path defect for shipping.
Output: Additive schema migration (new purchased_credits column with integrity CHECK), free-first consumption in deductCredit, a reset that only restores usage, and TDD coverage pinning all of it.
</objective>
