<objective>
Make StoreKit purchases actually resolve and complete end-to-end, and keep entitlement in sync with reality.

Purpose: The app ships an IAP UI whose purchase path is dead in every real build: `StoreKitServiceEnvironment.swift` references a `#if DEBUG`-only type unconditionally (Release does not compile), and `project.pbxproj` carries zero `STOREKIT_PREMIUM_*_PRODUCT_ID` build settings so `StoreKitProductCatalog.live.allProductIDs` is always empty, every purchase throws `missingProductConfiguration`, and the paywall shows hardcoded mock prices. The entitlement-wiring half is already correct (`Transaction.updates` owned at app scope, `refreshEntitlements()` on `scenePhase == .active`) but unverified by tests. Display honesty is broken in two spots: `PlanCard` hardcodes "Save 37%" when real savings are unknown, and the trial banner shows to users who already exhausted their intro offer. Premium character re-locking disagrees with the one-time-permanent product decision. This plan fixes the Release-compile blocker and product-ID wiring first (the tracer — a Release build that resolves real product IDs), then expands to display honesty, the character-entitlement decision, and the cancel/expiry + CI-guard tests.

Output: A Release-compiling StoreKit environment, real product IDs in build settings, the scheme wired to the local `.storekit` session, honest price/savings/trial rendering, one-time-permanent character unlocks, and a test suite that verifies purchase/restore/cancel/expiry plus guards CI against an empty product catalog.

**User story (from ROADMAP Phase 4 Goal):** **As a** premium-inclined user, **I want** to complete a purchase that actually works and have my premium features reflect my real subscription state, **so that** I am never charged without receiving entitlement and never locked out of what I paid for.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| App ↔ StoreKit (on-device) | Purchase/restore/entitlement calls cross into the StoreKit system service; a tampered receipt or sandbox glitch can return unexpected states. |
| App ↔ App Store Connect (external, lead-time) | Product/subscription-group definitions live server-side; until created, Release falls back to mock prices. |
| Paywall view ↔ PremiumState | The UI reads entitlement from `PremiumState.isPremiumUser`; a stale value gates features incorrectly until the next foreground refresh. |
